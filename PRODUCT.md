# PRODUCT

AegisGo is a location-aware real-time **risk awareness** platform. Daily situational awareness is the core. Travel Mode is a context, not a separate product.

## Goal

Help people discover nearby reported events, judge distance / time / impact / confidence, get relevant alerts, report in ~10–20 seconds, and confirm without being sent to a scene.

## Non-claims

Do not say “this place is safe” or “this place is dangerous.” Show known reports, when, how far, how well supported, whether still active, and how to avoid.

## Users

Primary: urban residents, night movement, commuters, students, people walking or using transit. Secondary: travelers, event attendees, families. Later: organizations (hotels, campuses, duty of care).

## Rules

- Report **what is happening**, not who someone is.
- No people / race / ethnicity / “looks suspicious” taxonomies.
- Confidence ≠ severity. No single danger score. Likes ≠ truth.
- Empty state: “No active incidents have been reported nearby recently.”
- Media, if added later, is private to moderation first.
- Core nearby safety information is never paywalled.

## MVP loop

Observe → Report → Screening → Nearby visibility → Community confirmation → Confidence update → Relevant alert → User action → Update → Resolution → Archive.

## Out of MVP

Chat, DMs, stranger tracking, face/plate databases, gamification, crypto, always-on chatbot, dispatch, global coverage, full B2B.

## Success

People open the app for nearby context, understand cards, finish reports quickly, confirm, trust multi-source better than one report, and are not trained into panic or notification fatigue.
